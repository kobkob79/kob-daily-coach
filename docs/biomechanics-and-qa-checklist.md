# Biomechanics and QA Checklist

## Process Requirement

Every exercise requires professional-source research before generation. AI generation alone does not constitute publication approval. Every asset (Hero Cover and Motion Video) must pass this checklist before publication.

## Verification Items

Before an exercise is switched to the V2 asset pipeline, verify the following:

- [ ] **Starting and ending positions:** Accurately represent the biomechanics of the exercise.
- [ ] **Grip and equipment setup:** Correctly positioned and scaled.
- [ ] **Joint alignment:** Anatomically sound and safe.
- [ ] **Resistance path:** The line of pull or push matches real-world physics for the equipment.
- [ ] **Safe range of motion:** Does not depict hyperextension or dangerous joint loading.
- [ ] **Controlled tempo:** Motion Video displays a realistic, controlled speed of execution.
- [ ] **Primary and secondary muscles:** Correctly identified and highlighted (orange/light orange).
- [ ] **Hero/video identity consistency:** The demonstrator is visually identical across the static and motion assets.
- [ ] **Equipment consistency:** Equipment does not change shape, size, or style during the motion.
- [ ] **Seamless loop:** The Motion Video loops without visible jumps or resets.
