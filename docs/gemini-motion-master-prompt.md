# Gemini Motion Master Prompt Template

## Objective

Generate a seamless looping Motion Video of a single exercise repetition from an approved static Hero Cover.

## Strict Motion Lock Requirements

To maintain continuity and visual integrity with the Hero Cover, the generated motion video must strictly adhere to the following constraints:

1. **Identity & Appearance:**
   - Same identity, face, and anatomy as the Hero Cover.
   - Same clothing.
   - Same anatomical styling (grey body, orange primary muscles, light orange secondary muscles).
2. **Environment & Setup:**
   - Same equipment and attachments.
   - Same camera, framing, lighting, and background.
3. **Camera Movement:**
   - **No zoom, pan, or creative camera movement.** The camera must remain absolutely static.
4. **Permitted Movement:**
   - Only required joints and mechanical equipment may move.
5. **Execution:**
   - One controlled repetition.
   - Seamless loop (start and end frames match exactly).
   - Duration target: 6–10 seconds.
